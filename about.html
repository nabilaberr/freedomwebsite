ABOUT FREEDOM RECYCLING

Turning plastic waste into premium recycled PET flakes

Founded in 2020 in Berrouaghia, Algeria, Freedom Recycling specializes in recycling post-consumer and post-industrial PET bottles into high-quality materials for industry.

CONTACT US

Who we are

Freedom Recycling is dedicated to transforming plastic waste into valuable resources for a cleaner environment and a more sustainable future.

What we produce

We produce premium recycled PET flakes that meet high industry standards for consistent quality and purity.

Where they are used

Our flakes are used in textiles, food packaging, and beverage packaging applications.

TECHNOLOGY

Advanced optical sorting for purity and quality

We use Bühler optical sorting technology, including two SORTEX A units and one SORTEX N unit, to achieve precise color separation and effective contaminant removal.

Circular economy

Every bottle we recycle helps reduce plastic pollution, conserve natural resources, and give plastic a second life.
