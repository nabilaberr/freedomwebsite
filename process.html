PROCESS

Controlled and Reliable Production

Each stage of our process is carefully managed to ensure the quality of recycled materials and the consistency of every shipment.

Collection

Plastic waste is collected responsibly from post-consumer and industrial sources.

Sorting

Materials are separated through a combination of manual inspection and advanced mechanical sorting.

Washing

Impurities are removed through an efficient hot-washing process to achieve high material purity.

Grinding

The cleaned material is processed into uniform flakes for further recycling.

Extrusion

The flakes are transformed into high-quality recycled pellets suitable for industrial applications.

Quality Control

Every batch undergoes final quality checks before shipment to ensure compliance with customer requirements.
