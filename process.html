OUR PROCESS

Controlled production. Reliable results.

Each stage of our process is carefully managed to ensure high-quality recycled materials and consistency in every shipment.

HOW IT WORKS

From plastic waste to valuable materials

Our integrated recycling process transforms collected plastic waste into high-quality recycled flakes and pellets.

01

Collection

Plastic waste is collected responsibly from post-consumer and industrial sources.

02

Sorting

Materials are separated through manual inspection and advanced mechanical sorting.

03

Washing

Impurities are removed through an efficient hot-washing process for high material purity.

04

Grinding

Cleaned material is processed into uniform flakes for further recycling.

05

Extrusion

Flakes are transformed into high-quality recycled pellets for industrial applications.

06

Quality Control

Every batch undergoes final quality checks before shipment to meet customer requirements.
