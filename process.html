PROCESS

Our Recycling Process

Every step is organized to guarantee the quality of recycled materials and consistent shipments.

01

Collection

Plastic waste collected responsibly from industrial and commercial sources.

02

Sorting

Manual and mechanical separation of materials by type and quality.

03

Washing

Removal of impurities with hot water washing and cleaning.

04

Crushing

Transformation into homogeneous flakes for further processing.

05

Extrusion

Conversion into recycled pellets/granules for industrial use.

06

Quality Control

Final quality checks before shipment to ensure consistency.

WHY IT MATTERS

Transparent, traceable recycling

We track materials from collection to final product, ensuring sustainability and quality.

Our process supports the circular economy by transforming waste into valuable raw materials for manufacturing.
