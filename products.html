Recycled Plastic Materials

High-quality recycled PET products for industrial manufacturing and sustainable packaging applications.

Recycled PET Flakes

Our recycled PET flakes are produced through an advanced and environmentally responsible recycling process. Sourced from post-consumer and post-industrial PET plastic bottles, the material is carefully sorted, washed, and processed to achieve high purity standards. Versatile and sustainable, these flakes are widely used in food-grade films, food trays, textile fibers, packaging, and many other industrial applications.

Transparent PET Preform Regrind

Our transparent PET preform regrind provides a reliable and versatile solution for a wide range of manufacturing applications. Produced from recycled PET preforms, it offers excellent transparency, consistent quality, and dependable performance for packaging, injection-molded products, and other industrial uses

Light Blue PET Preform Regrind

Freedom Recycling’s light blue PET preform regrind is produced from high-quality recycled PET preforms. This material is ideal for applications requiring a distinctive color while delivering the environmental benefits of recycling. It offers consistent quality and reliable performance for a variety of industrial manufacturing processes.

PET Flakes Colored

Our colored PET flakes are carefully sorted and processed to deliver reliable recycled material for industrial use. They are suitable for a range of manufacturing applications where color consistency and recycled content are important.
