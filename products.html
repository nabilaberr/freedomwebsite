Recycled PET Flakes

Our recycled PET flakes are produced through an advanced and environmentally responsible recycling process. Sourced from post-consumer and post-industrial PET bottles, the material is carefully sorted, washed, and processed to achieve high purity standards. These versatile flakes are widely used in food-grade films, food trays, textile fibers, packaging, and other industrial applications.

Transparent PET Preform Regrind

Our transparent PET preform regrind provides a reliable and versatile solution for a wide range of manufacturing applications. Produced from recycled PET preforms, it offers excellent transparency, consistent quality, and dependable performance for packaging, injection-molded products, and other industrial uses.

Light Blue PET Preform Regrind

Freedom Recycling’s light blue PET preform regrind is produced from high-quality recycled PET preforms. This material is ideal for applications requiring a distinctive color while delivering the environmental benefits of recycling. It offers consistent quality and reliable performance for a variety of industrial manufacturing processes.
