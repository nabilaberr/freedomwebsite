Our Mission

Freedom Recycling transforms post-consumer and post-industrial PET bottle waste into high-quality recycled raw materials.

Based in Berrouaghia, Algeria, our company operates a strong infrastructure dedicated to collection, sorting, washing, and recycling.

Through rigorous recycling processes, we produce recycled PET flakes for industrial applications.

These flakes are used by our clients to manufacture food films, trays, textile fibers, and many other products, reducing dependence on virgin resources.

Freedom Recycling — Turning today’s waste into tomorrow’s resources.
